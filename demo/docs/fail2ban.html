With fail2ban

They do different jobs. Run both.

fail2bansshsentry
Blocks IPsYesNo (detection only)
Tells you a login succeeded after failuresNoYes (HIGH / CRITICAL)
New user, new SSH key, new IP/countryNoYes
Plain-English alerts, daily digestNoYes

A sensible small-server setup: key-only SSH (PasswordAuthentication no), fail2ban's sshd jail, a firewall, and sshsentry to tell you when something gets past them or changes underneath you.

Both read the same auth log and don't interfere with each other. fail2ban bans may mean you see fewer brute-force alerts, which is fine: the alerts that remain are the interesting ones.
